# Website design plan

Subject: Nepal road-pavement decisions, for interested citizens and infrastructure decision makers.
Primary job: explain a conditional recommendation and expose its assumptions, costs, tradeoffs and evidence.

Palette: cloud #edf3f6; ink #17354b; asphalt #425564; river #147f8a; concrete #c3cfce; road marking #e8b44f.
Typography: Aptos / Segoe UI / system sans; heavier, tightly spaced large headlines and quiet readable body; tabular numerals for comparison.
Layout: left-aligned analytical narrative, open whitespace, one memorable custom illustrated terrain-and-road diagram. Sticky section navigation. Different structures for principles, comparison, cost chart, matrix and timeline. No stock-photo hero or identical card grid.

Sketch:
  navigation                                      evidence/download
  large plain-language thesis   |   illustrated road through terrain
  qualified recommendation      |   selectable asphalt/concrete segments
  statement + evidence status
  road cross-section            |   cause → failure → intervention
  three-column comparable alternatives
  assumption controls           |   whole-life cost plot + cost table
  adjustable weighted matrix, explicitly subjective
  staged rollout with decision gates / resource budget
  searchable evidence and reproducible downloads

Review against brief: the illustration teaches segment selection, not generic scenery. The model is reproducible, and the matrix can contradict the recommended policy under different priorities. Blue-grey and road-marking yellow come from road materials; no gratuitous animation. Mobile uses stacked charts with readable labels; tables scroll within their containers. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion and print styles required.

Research cutoff: 18 October 2026. Evidence, model assumptions and recommendations must be visually distinguishable. Quantities are conceptual budgeting quantities, never issued-for-construction details.
