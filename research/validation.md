# Validation and limits

Delivery check: 19 October 2026. Evidence cutoff: 18 October 2026.

## Model

- Python standard-library reference model and browser JavaScript agree in 834 accounting/parity checks across the baseline and 243 deterministic scenarios.
- Independent review reconciled annual discounted cashflows, upfront budget components, the 150 mm DLC update and the selection scores.
- Baseline per-km agency present values: A NPR 55.308935m, B NPR 60.700807m, C NPR 53.976794m.
- The four-to-five-year stressed asphalt renewal sensitivity reverses the baseline cost preference. The deterministic grid is not a probability distribution.
- Pilot arithmetic is separate from the 10 km demonstration: NPR 428.76m subtotal, 64.314m contingency and 64.09962m VAT cash reserve = NPR 557.17362m, or 55.72 crore.

## Website

- Browser inspection in the Codex in-app browser at narrow-panel and wider-panel layouts; no page-wide horizontal overflow was observed in the inspected wider layout. Tables retain their own scrolling region.
- Verified diagram explanation opening, pinned popup persistence, Escape dismissal and keyboard opening of a cost-bar explanation.
- Verified the five-year renewal scenario selects asphalt and Reset assumptions restores the baseline portfolio result. Fixed the native-select reset behavior by explicitly restoring all defaults.
- Verified the budget/access matrix preset selects A and the domestic-supply/maintenance preset selects B; baseline selects C.
- Verified source search filters the register. The standalone HTML includes the data, solver and styling; external references/downloads require connectivity.
- Hover behavior is implemented with a dismissible, pointer-accessible popup; click/tap and keyboard pinning are alternatives. Dedicated touchscreen-device and cross-browser testing were not performed.

## What validation does not prove

No field investigation, equivalent structural design, market quotation survey, deterioration calibration or environmental inventory was performed. The study is a reproducible decision-screening analysis. Current procurement and project-specific design requirements must be checked before implementation. The pilot is proposed, not committed by any agency.
